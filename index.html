About the foundation

Making kindness useful through action.

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organisation based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. Its work focuses on community development and social empowerment, with an emphasis on education, support for women, animal care, environmental awareness and help for people in need.

The foundation's message is simple: when people contribute their time, skills and care, small efforts can become meaningful support for others.

Our purpose: to encourage a society where people have more opportunities to learn, grow and live with dignity.

What the initiatives focus on

Six projects, one shared purpose

Each initiative responds to a different need. Together, they encourage compassion, learning and a healthier environment.

Bachpanshala

Supports children through learning opportunities and educational resources, helping them build confidence and a stronger foundation for the future.

Seva

Focuses on helping people in need through essentials such as food and clothing, while promoting dignity and community support.

Jeev

Promotes care and compassion for animals, including support for animals that need food, protection or help.

Udaan

Encourages women's empowerment through skills, resources and opportunities that can support greater confidence and independence.

Prakriti

Promotes environmental responsibility through tree planting, awareness and everyday actions that help protect nature.

Vikas

Focuses on youth development and skill-building, helping young people explore their abilities and prepare for future opportunities.
